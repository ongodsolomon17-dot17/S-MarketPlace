ABOUT S-MARKET PLACE

A marketplace built around independent traders.

S-Market Place brings products from independent shops into one place. Buyers can discover items across shops, while traders manage their own listings, orders, and earnings through one account.

HOW IT WORKS

One marketplace, two ways to take part.

⌕

FOR BUYERS

Find products across shops

Browse without an account, search by product or category, compare trader listings, and keep items in your cart. A buyer account is needed to place and manage an order.

▣

FOR TRADERS

Run your shop in one place

Create a storefront, list and update products, manage stock, post time-limited Fast Finger deals, and follow paid orders through fulfilment.

↗

FOR EVERY ORDER

Clear payment and delivery steps

Paystack processes checkout. Order records show payment and delivery progress, and buyers can confirm receipt or report a problem through the order flow.

01

Discover

Search the marketplace, explore categories, or open a trader’s shop to see its products.

02

Review your cart and complete checkout through Paystack. Your order is then available in your account.

03

Confirm delivery

Follow delivery updates. Confirm receipt when your order arrives, or report a problem through the order dispute flow.

PAYMENTS & ACCOUNT SAFETY

Know what happens after checkout.

Checkout payments are handled by Paystack. For eligible orders, seller proceeds are held until delivery is confirmed or a reported issue is reviewed. Traders can see their balance breakdown and request an available payout to their saved bank account.

Accounts use sign-in credentials and role-based access for buyer, trader, and admin actions. Admins can enable authenticator-code sign-in checks. Keep your password and verification codes private, and complete purchases through the marketplace checkout.

Traders can view and edit existing products after their trial or paid access ends. Adding products and marked Pro tools require an active trial or subscription. Current plan choices and prices appear in the trader account centre.
